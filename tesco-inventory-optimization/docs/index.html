Read the model honestly

Training: January–September 2025. Holdout: October–December 2025. No policy sees future demand or realised delivery delays. Forecasts are fixed-origin benchmarks; replenishment uses a training mean. £2 per SKU-store order and 20% annual holding cost are illustrative assumptions. No transport consolidation, substitution, minimum order quantities or terminal stock valuation is modelled.
